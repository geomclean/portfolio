Hello! I'm George McLean (they/them)

I am a musician, sound artist, audio recordist, experienced promoter and have been part of a number of bands. Looking to get involved in more sound-based projects and can bring lots of experience to the table!

Member of synth rock band Astrid Von Der Pahlen as lead guitar and drum guitar

We also write regularly about what we are up to on our substack here

In the three years we have existed as Astrid, we have had several members and iterations that have come and gone, but we're now a 5 piece band (with a trumpet, drum machine, synth, bass, guitar, vocals, and a melodica!) and an immersive visual artist.

I was bassist in Save Face, and played in legendary Glasgow and Edinburgh venues King Tuts Wah Wah Hut and the Voodoo Rooms. Organised and promoted our own sell out show at Banshee Labyrinth. Recorded and released 6 original songs, which received national and international radio play, and was playlisted on an official Spotify playlist.

Experienced Promoter Representative at 432 Presents, supporting artists such as Getdown Services, This Is The Kit, and Bears In Trees, in established Edinburgh venues La Belle Angele, Mash House, and The Voodoo Rooms

Sound Recordist at Neon Eye, capturing live singing, instrumentation, and spoken word during Edinburgh Fringe 2026.

Founder and Organiser of GAFF, mutual-aid fundraising group for trans and non-binary people in Edinburgh. Facilitated 7 sell-out gigs and events, with an all trans and non-binary lineup, focussing on creating safe and accessible spaces, with all funds going to local people on a no-strings-attached basis.
